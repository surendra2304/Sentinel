## Key Decisions & Architecture
- Evidence-First Enforcement: An observation without attached evidence artifacts is strictly rejected.
- Composite Finding Deduplication: Findings for the same asset and issue are merged, updating confidence and history.
- Contextual Risk Tiers: Risk scores scale based on internet exposure and asset criticality rather than static CVSS.
- Tamper-Evident Forensics: Every single evidence read appends an immutable audit custody record.
- Autonomous Risk Recalculation: Dynamic triggers update task risk rollups in real-time as new findings land.
