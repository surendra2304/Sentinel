## Bugs I Found & Fixed
- **Route f-string Single Braces**: FRIDAY GET/POST route paths used `{delegation_id}` instead of `{{delegation_id}}`.
  - *Fix*: Added double braces so FastAPI path template resolves correctly at registration time.
- **ApprovalRecord.target Attribute**: Used non-existent `.target` instead of `.target_refs` on ApprovalRecord.
  - *Fix*: Changed to `", ".join(a.target_refs)` to correctly join the list field into a single string.
- **Mid-file Module Imports**: FRIDAY imports were appended below routes violating PEP 8 and ruff E402.
  - *Fix*: Moved all imports to the top of main.py and ran `ruff check --fix` to auto-sort the block.
- **mypy no-any-return**: FridayClient methods returned untyped `res.json()` Any without explicit cast.
  - *Fix*: Added `cast(dict[str, Any], res.json())` to all four async client methods.

## Key Decisions & Architecture
- Governance Boundary: Sentinel enforces its OWN scope and policy regardless of FRIDAY policy_context.
- Advisory Input: FRIDAY policy_context (environment, auth_reference) is metadata, not a policy override.
- Zero-LLM Summarizer: Deterministic templating means the FRIDAY handoff works offline with no model.
- Blocked Action Transparency: FRIDAY result contract mandates blocked_actions visibility at schema level.
